# 16 — Future Ideas (not in the current rollout)

> **Status**: Ideas backlog · **Last Updated**: 2026-09-26  
> Back to [feature overview](README.md)

Ideas that build on the Pet ID but wait until the registry, health passport and verified network
are live.

| Idea | What it is | Depends on | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| DNA breed tests | Owner or breeder orders a cheek-swab kit from a lab partner; result (breed mix, genetic health markers) attached to the Pet ID as a verified document | Registry, lab partner, product catalog | Raises trust in pedigree claims; breeders could show "DNA verified parents". Lab partner to be researched |
| Nose-print photo matching | Match a dog's nose-print photo against stored prints to identify a found dog without a chip | Nose prints collected at registration ([02](02-pet-identity-marks.md)) | Needs a matching model and enough prints; supporting evidence only, never proof of ownership |
| Pet birthday offers | On the pet's birthday, a greeting plus a brand sample or discount | Registry DOB, `BRAND_OFFERS` consent, campaigns ([04](04-data-insights-and-brands.md)) | Only for owners who opted in; uses species/breed/age only, never health data |
| Pet memorial page | Optional page after a pet dies, with photos and messages | `DECEASED` status | Owner-controlled; no ads |
| Breeding match | Verified breeders find suitable mates for their pets by breed, lineage and health record | Registry lineage, breeder score | Must block close-relative matches using the family tree |
| Travel documents | Pet travel checklist and certificate pack built from the passport | Health passport | Rules differ per airline and country |
