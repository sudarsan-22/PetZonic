# 08 — Indian Suppliers, Partners & Pricing

> **Status**: Research + proposal · **Researched**: 2026-09-26 (prices as published on that date)  
> Back to [feature overview](README.md)

India already has custom leg-ring makers, microchip suppliers at about ₹1,200–1,500 per pet,
and a microchip registry with 900+ partner vets. PetZonic should **partner, not build hardware**.

---

| [VoliKart](https://www.volikart.com/collections/bird-leg-ring) | Online | Closed aluminium rings 4.5–6.7 mm, custom name/phone printing | ₹399–449 per 10 (~₹40–45/ring); many sizes sold out | Backup, retail benchmark |

Ring code must fit Avigene's 16-character limit: `PZ-TN-2026-000123` is 17 characters, so rings
carry the compact `PZTN26000123` (12 characters).

## 3. Vet network for microchipping

- **Clinic chains**: [Vetic](https://www.jswvc.com/vetic) (Gurugram, founded 2022, describes
  itself as India's largest vet clinic chain) — first chain to approach.
- **City programmes**: the [Greater Chennai Corporation](https://www.dtnext.in/news/chennai/chennai-corporation-makes-microchip-mandatory-for-pet-dogs-rs-3000-fine-for-violations-845540)
  made dog microchipping mandatory from October 2025, procured 2 lakh chips and briefed private
  and government vet hospitals. Import these numbers rather than compete.
- **PetZonic's own vets**: verified `VET_CONSULTATION` providers become **PetZonic ID points**,
  paid per chip scanned or implanted.

**Constraint**: phones cannot read 134.2 kHz ISO chips — a dedicated scanner is required.

## 5. Proposed PetZonic prices

To be tested with 10 shops and 3 brands before launch.

| Product | Proposed price | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Basic Verified badge | Free for 6 months, then ₹2,999/year | Covers video call + document review; below TrustSEAL's lowest tier |
| Verified Plus | ₹9,999/year | Priority ranking, featured near-me slot, district insights; field visit included |
| Bird ring pack (10 rings with Pet IDs) | ₹499 | ~₹50/ring, close to retail custom rings; registration included |
| Microchip at a PetZonic ID point | ₹1,499 incl. GST; vet partner keeps an agreed share | Matches Whizzles / Pet Chip India |
| Targeted banners & placements | ₹150 per 1,000 impressions | ~2.5× Justdial's untargeted rate, justified by breed + district targeting |
| Sponsored push or email | ₹1.50 per delivered message; SMS at cost + 30% | Consenting owners only |
| State breed-and-district report | ₹49,999 per report | Narrower than global reports (USD 2,450–3,599) but unique data |
| All-India insights subscription | ₹4,99,999/year | Quarterly national data + dashboard |
