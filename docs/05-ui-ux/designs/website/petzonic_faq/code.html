How can we help?

Find answers to frequently asked questions about PetZonic.

search

Buying a pet is simple and secure. Browse our listed pets, use filters to find the right breed and age, and click "Enquire" or "Buy Now". You'll be connected with the verified seller to discuss health certificates, vaccination records, and delivery options before completing the transaction through our secure portal.

Yes, absolutely. We have a multi-step verification process. Every seller must provide government ID, proof of residence, and if they are a breeder, their kennel license. Look for the "Verified" Teal badge on profiles for the highest level of trust and security.

Listing a pet is currently free for individual pet parents! Professional breeders can choose from our 'Standard' or 'Premium' monthly subscription plans which offer higher visibility and advanced analytics for their listings.

We partner with climate-controlled, pet-certified logistics providers. Once a purchase is confirmed, you can choose "PetZonic Transport". Our team handles the pickup, ensures the pet has a comfortable journey with scheduled breaks, and delivers them right to your doorstep with real-time tracking.

PetZonic has a dedicated "Adoption Centers" section. You can browse pets from local shelters, donate directly through their verified profiles, or sign up to be a volunteer. 100% of donations made through our platform go directly to the respective animal welfare organizations.
