Best food for a 3-month-old Labrador puppy?

Hey everyone! I just brought home my 3-month-old Labrador puppy, Bruno. He's full of energy but I'm really confused about the right diet for his growth stage. Labradors are known for having joint issues later in life, so I want to make sure he's getting the right balance of calcium and phosphorus right now.

Currently, I'm looking at a few high-protein kibble brands that specify "large breed puppy" formulas. Does anyone have experience with Royal Canin vs. Orijen for Labs? I've heard mixed things about grain-free diets for this breed specifically.

Also, how many times a day should I be feeding him? Any advice on healthy toppers like pumpkin or yogurt would be greatly appreciated! Here's a photo of the little guy for tax!

Dr. Aman verified VERIFIED VET 2h ago

Congratulations on Bruno! For Labradors, the calcium-to-phosphorus ratio is critical to prevent Hip Dysplasia. Stick to 'Large Breed' specific puppy food as it has lower energy density and controlled minerals. Royal Canin is excellent for bone development, but avoid grain-free unless he shows specific allergies (rare in pups).
