The Golden Standard of Companionship

The Golden Retriever is one of the most popular dog breeds in India, and for good reason. Originally bred in Scotland for retrieving game, their "soft mouth" and eager-to-please nature transitioned perfectly into the role of the ultimate family pet.

"A Golden Retriever doesn't just join your family; they become the heart of it. Their intuitive nature makes them excellent therapy dogs and patient playmates for children."

Comprehensive Care Guide

brush

Grooming Needs

Brush at least 3 times a week to manage heavy shedding, especially during seasonal changes in the Indian climate.

directions_run

Daily Activity

Needs 60-90 minutes of active play. Fetch, swimming, and long walks in the early morning or late evening are ideal.

home

Living Space

Can adapt to apartments if exercised well, but thrive in homes with yards. They strictly need indoor air-conditioned spaces in Indian summers.

health_and_safety

Vet Visits

Quarterly deworming and annual vaccinations are mandatory. Regular ear cleaning is essential to prevent infections.

warning

Common Health Concerns

  • Hip & Elbow Dysplasia
  • Progressive Retinal Atrophy (PRA)
  • Heart Disease (Subvalvular Aortic Stenosis)
  • Hypothyroidism
  • Skin Allergies (Hot Spots)
  • Obesity (If overfed)

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Golden Retrievers good for apartment living in India? expand_more
Yes, they can adapt well to apartment living if they receive at least 60-90 minutes of daily physical exercise and mental stimulation. However, they are sensitive to the Indian heat and must be kept in cool, indoor environments during peak sun hours.
Do they shed a lot? expand_more
Yes, Golden Retrievers are heavy shedders. They "blow" their coat twice a year during seasonal changes. Regular brushing (3-4 times a week) is essential to manage loose hair and maintain coat health.
How much does a puppy cost in Mumbai or Delhi? expand_more
In major metros like Mumbai or Delhi, a healthy Golden Retriever puppy from a reputable, KCI-registered breeder can cost between ₹25,000 and ₹45,000. Avoid "backyard" breeders selling at suspiciously low prices.
