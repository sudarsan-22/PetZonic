How PetZonic Was Born

Our journey started in 2018 when we realized the difficulty of finding ethical breeders and healthy companions in India. Too many pet parents were facing heartbreak due to lack of transparency and poor health standards.

We built PetZonic to bridge that gap—a platform where trust is the primary currency. By vetting every seller and supporting every buyer, we've created a safe haven for India's growing pet family. Our commitment is to joyful reliability, ensuring that the bond between you and your new companion starts on the best possible foundation.
