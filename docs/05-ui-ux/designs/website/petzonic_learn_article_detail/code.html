Complete Puppy Vaccination Schedule in India

Dr. Anita Rao

Oct 24, 2024 • 8 min read

In this article

Bringing home a puppy is one of life's greatest joys. However, it also comes with the immense responsibility of ensuring their health and longevity. In India, where tropical climates can accelerate the spread of certain canine diseases, a rigorous vaccination schedule isn't just recommended—it's essential.

lightbulb

Pro Tip

Always wait at least 7 days after bringing your puppy home before their first shot. This allows their immune system to settle after the stress of relocation.

Core vs Non-Core Vaccines

Vaccines are generally categorized into "Core"—those that every dog must have—and "Non-Core"—those that are optional based on lifestyle, location, and risk factors.

  • DHLPP (Core): Protects against Distemper, Hepatitis, Leptospirosis, Parainfluenza, and Parvovirus.
  • Anti-Rabies (Core): Mandatory by law in India.
  • Canine Influenza (Non-Core): Recommended for dogs in boarding or daycare.

The 2024 Vaccination Schedule

The following schedule is recommended by leading veterinarians across major Indian metros like Delhi, Mumbai, and Bangalore.

Puppy Age Recommended Vaccines
6-8 Weeks DHLPP (1st Dose) + Deworming
9-11 Weeks DHLPP (2nd Dose) + Corona Virus
12-14 Weeks DHLPP (3rd Dose) + Anti-Rabies (1st Dose)
15-16 Weeks Anti-Rabies Booster + Kennel Cough
