What to do right now

1

Report Immediately

The first few hours are critical. Post on PetZonic, tell your neighbors, and notify local animal shelters and vets.

2

Leave Scent Markers

Place your pet's bed or a piece of your worn clothing outside your door. The familiar scent can guide them back home.

3

Check Microchips

Ensure your contact information is up to date in the national microchip database so rescuers can reach you.
